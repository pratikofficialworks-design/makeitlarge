Plain English Explanation

What's a Static Website?

Think of your website like a digital visiting card — but one that shows up on Google when someone searches for your business.


A static website is like a beautifully designed board outside your shop — it shows your services, prices, photos, location, and contact. Customers see it, they call or WhatsApp you, and you do business. Simple.


You don't need online payments, a shopping cart, or anything complicated. Most small businesses just need to be found online — and that's exactly what a static site does, perfectly.

Things to Know

Before You Start

Here's what to expect. We believe in being honest about how websites work, so you're not surprised later.

⏱️
Google Ranking Takes Time

A new website won't show up on Google search immediately. It typically takes 4–12 weeks for Google to index your site. Ranking for competitive keywords can take months. But here's the good news — the link works instantly. You can share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, or word of mouth from day one.

🌐
Custom Domain is Optional

By default, your site gets a free link like yourbusiness.netlify.app when you deploy it. If you want a professional yourbusiness.com address, it costs around ₹1,500–2,000 per year. A custom domain also helps slightly with Google visibility over time.

🤝
Website Alone Isn't Magic

A website makes your business look professional and findable online. But it works best combined with Google Maps listing, WhatsApp sharing, and word of mouth. I'll guide you on setting up a free Google Business Profile after your site is live.
